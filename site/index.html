You are on a call. You wander off into other windows. Someone asks you a question — and the meeting tab is nowhere to be found. QuickJump keeps it one click away.

Small bar, big shortcut

Everything you pinned, always in view, never in the way.

Add from the right-click menu

Right-click any page and choose Add to Quick Jump. Right-click a link to save a page you have not opened yet.

One click to jump

Chrome activates the tab, un-minimises its window and raises it, wherever it is.

Floats above every window

Uses Chrome's Picture-in-Picture to stay on top of other apps on Linux, macOS and Windows. No setup.

Auto-hides

Out of the way while Chrome is focused, back the moment you switch to another app.

Survives closed tabs

A closed tab stays in the list, dimmed, and reopens with one click. Entries also survive a browser restart.

Yours to arrange

Drag to reorder. Remove with × or a middle-click. New items land on top, or at the bottom if you prefer.

Right-click. Add. Done.

Pin the call before you wander off. The entry keeps the page's title and icon, so the list stays easy to scan.

  • Add to Quick Jump: the current page
  • Add link to Quick Jump: a link, without opening it
  • Show Quick Jump bar: bring the bar back

Always on top, no setup

Open the bar from the toolbar. Always on top (PiP) floats it above every app through Chrome's own Picture-in-Picture window. Prefer a regular window? Floating bar opens one; on KDE Plasma a ready-made window rule pins it.

Go further with the desktop agent

A small, free companion app. Optional: the extension works fine without it.

  • One window for every profile: all Chrome profiles and Chromium browsers, with a filter for each
  • Global hotkeys: Alt+Shift+Q then 1–9 from any app; Alt+Shift+R for the newest item
  • Tray icon: one click jumps to the newest item
  • Works when the browser is closed: starts the right profile at the saved page

Up and running in a minute

No account, no configuration.

Install the extension

Add QuickJump to Chrome. It works on its own right away.

Pin what matters

Right-click a page → Add to Quick Jump. Open the bar from the toolbar icon.

Optional: run the agent

For global hotkeys, a tray icon and one window for all your profiles. The extension finds it automatically.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no servers. Your list lives in your browser's local storage. If you run the agent, the extension talks only to it, on your own computer (127.0.0.1). Nothing leaves your machine.

The code is open source under the MIT license, so you can check every line. Read the privacy policy.
